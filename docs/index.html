Smart Highlights How it picks the plays

A whole season of GameChanger clips, cut down to the plays that matter.

Paste your player's GameChanger link and pick your YouTube channel. Smart Highlights finds the key moment in every clip — the crack of the bat, the strike-three pitch — trims to the windup and the result, adds a caption, and uploads one reel to your channel.

The reel service isn't running yet, so reels can't be made from this page right now. You can still read how it works below, or run the tool on your own computer from the GitHub repository.

    How it picks the plays
    
    
    
      
    
        
    The bat tells you when
    
        
    Bat on ball is the loudest sound in a clip, hundreds of times louder than the crowd. Fouls are loud too, so the last big crack before the play was scored is the one put in play.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Strike three comes after a still moment
    
        
    Everyone sets before a pitch. The last glove pop right after that stillness is the strike-three pitch, not the noise of teams walking off after the third out.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    The scorebook fills in the rest
    
        
    GameChanger's play-by-play says whether the at-bat ended in play or on strike three, and gives the caption its sentence: “triples on a fly ball to left fielder.”
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Trimmed to the windup and the result
    
        
    Each clip starts three seconds before the key moment and ends when the field settles — the runner on base, the out made. Blank or frozen clips are left out.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What happens to your information
    
    
      
      
    • Your GameChanger password is used for your reel and then discarded. It's never written to disk or logged.
      • 
      
    • The YouTube connection only lets the service upload to the channel you chose, and it's forgotten when your reel is done.
      • 
      
    • Downloaded clips are deleted when the reel is uploaded. Progress pages are removed after 7 days.
      • 
      
    • You need a GameChanger account that can see the player's clips, and the right to post them.
      • 
      
    • Smart Highlights isn't affiliated with or endorsed by GameChanger or YouTube.
